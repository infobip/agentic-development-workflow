% Why
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
  \raggedright
  \sectionhead{\textcolor{agent}{Agents write code faster}\\than humans can review it}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Losing track of the agent costs. A 25\% rise in AI adoption came with an estimated 7.2\% drop in delivery stability~\cite{dora2024}. One agent deleted a production database during a code freeze~\cite{sharwood2025replit}.
    \item Watching is not enough. Operators who only monitor lose the skill to take over~\cite{bainbridge1983ironies}.
    \item Autopilot kept the pilot, who works hardest at takeoff and landing. Our workflow puts human effort in the same places.
  \end{itemize}
\end{minipage}%
\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
  \raggedright
  \sectionhead{Long, noisy context\\makes agents worse}
  Multi-turn chats cut LLM performance by {\bfseries 39\%} on average~\cite{laban2025lostturn}. Irrelevant context lowers accuracy even when the answer is present~\cite{hong2025contextrot}.\par

\takeaway{Front-load the thinking.}
  {Humans own research and planning, where mistakes cost the most.}%

\takeaway{Build the backpressure.}
  {Acceptance criteria and validation reject bad output before you review it.}%

\takeaway{Stay on the loop.}
  {One task per fresh session. You review, and bad tasks go back.}

\panel{0.485\textwidth}{\bottomh}{%
  \sectionhead{Let's talk about open problems}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item {\bfseries No metrics for workflow effectiveness.} Nobody can yet show that a phased workflow beats unstructured delegation, or that tasks have the right size.\par
      \vspace{14pt}
      Candidate metrics, none tested yet\par
      \vspace{10pt}
      \begin{tabular}{@{}l@{}}
        \chip[failure]{task success rate per session}\\[8pt]
        \chip[failure]{instruction correction rate}\\[8pt]
        \chip[failure]{context utilization at task completion}
      \end{tabular}
    \item {\bfseries Research stops at components.} Pruning, compression, and tool selection are studied one at a time. The workflows that combine them have no shared terms, so teams cannot compare them.
  \end{enumerate}
  \vspace{28pt}
  {\bfseries Have a metric or a counterexample? Open an issue in the repo.\par}}
